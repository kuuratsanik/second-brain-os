Most weekly reviews fail for the same reason: they are too long. Mine takes
fifteen minutes and I do it on Friday, when the week is still in my head.
Three questions. What moved? What stalled? What is the one priority for next
week? Then I write the answer to the last question on a single line and stop.
When I let reviews run to an hour I skipped them within a month.
