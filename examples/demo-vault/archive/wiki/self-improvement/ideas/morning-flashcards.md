# Morning flashcards

> Fictional. This page belongs to the demo vault; the people, organisations and articles in it are invented.

## The idea

Turn the three recall sentences from each reading into flashcards and go through ten of them every morning.

## Where it came from

[[2026-09-14-oppimise-plaan|Õppimise plaan]], which recommends writing three sentences from memory after each reading.

## What supports it

- [[retrieval-practice|Retrieval practice]] in general.

## What argues against it

- Ten cards every morning is a daily task. The owner's journal of 2026-09-06 says earlier habit attempts stopped after three weeks, which is one data point against adding a daily habit.
- Neither retrieval source gives data on card-based practice.
