# Friday review trial, review

> Fictional. This page belongs to the demo vault; the people, organisations and articles in it are invented.

## What happened

Four Fridays, four completed reviews, taking 14, 17, 12 and 15 minutes (log in [[friday-review-trial|Friday review trial]]). On the three Mondays after the first three Fridays, the owner's journal names the week's priority correctly.

## Against the plan

- Success measure: all four Fridays done, each under twenty minutes, priority recalled on Monday. Met on all counts.
- Expectation: one skipped Friday. Not met; none were skipped. The expectation was wrong, and the owner writes that the deadline week was the easiest because the review took twelve minutes.

## What worked and what did not

Worked: the timer, and the single-line priority. Did not work: the second question (what stalled) was answered with one word three times out of four, so it adds little yet.

Limits: four weeks, one person, no comparison group. It does not show that the habit lasts, which was the claim in [[2026-08-12-fifteen-minute-weekly-review|the source]] that remains untested.

## Decision

Adopt, with the second question left in place for now. The routine is recorded as [[friday-review|Friday review]]. Check again at the end of the quarter.

## Next

Review again on 2026-12-18. If the second question is still answered in one word, adjust it. See also [[review-cadence|Review cadence]] for how recall practice might share the slot.
