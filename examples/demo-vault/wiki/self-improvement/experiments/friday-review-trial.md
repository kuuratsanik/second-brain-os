# Friday review trial

> Fictional. This page belongs to the demo vault; the people, organisations and articles in it are invented.

## The change

Every Friday at 16:30, a fifteen-minute review with a timer, using three questions: what moved, what stalled, what is next week's single priority. The answer to the last one is written on a single line.

## Why

Idea: [[fifteen-minute-friday-review|Fifteen-minute Friday review]]. Source: [[2026-08-12-fifteen-minute-weekly-review|The fifteen-minute weekly review]]. Concept: [[weekly-review|Weekly review]].

## Success measure

Written before the start date. Success means all four Fridays (2026-09-11, 09-18, 09-25 and 10-02) have a dated entry, each finished inside twenty minutes, and the owner can name the next week's priority from memory on the following Monday.

## What I expect

That one of the four Fridays will be skipped because of a deadline.
