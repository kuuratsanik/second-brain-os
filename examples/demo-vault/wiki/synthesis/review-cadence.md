# Review cadence

> Fictional. This page belongs to the demo vault; the people, organisations and articles in it are invented.

## The question

How often should the owner look back at material and at the week, and do the review habit and the recall habit want the same rhythm?

## What the sources say

- [[2026-08-12-fifteen-minute-weekly-review|The fifteen-minute weekly review]]: once a week, fifteen minutes, one decision.
- [[2026-09-03-retrieval-practice-at-work|Retrieval practice at work]]: recall attempts at one, three and ten days after first contact.
- [[2026-09-14-oppimise-plaan|Õppimise plaan]]: recall attempts at one, three and seven days.

## Where they disagree

The two retrieval sources differ by three days on the last attempt, and neither gives evidence for its figure. The weekly review source does not address recall at all. One weekly slot cannot also cover a one-day and a three-day recall.

## Current position

Keep the weekly review as a once-a-week slot ([[friday-review|Friday review]]) and treat recall as a separate, lighter habit tied to new reading, not to the calendar. The ten-day versus seven-day question is open; use seven days until a source with data says otherwise, because it fits the weekly slot.

## What would change it

A source that measures spacing for professionals, or the owner's own data from the [[friday-review-trial-review|trial review]] showing that Friday recall fits.
