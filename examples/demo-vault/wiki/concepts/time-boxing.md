# Time-boxing

> Fictional. This page belongs to the demo vault; the people, organisations and articles in it are invented.

## In one paragraph

Fixing the length of a task in advance and stopping when the time is up, whatever the state of the work. Used here for reviews and meetings so that they stay short enough to survive.

## Where it came from

The fifteen-minute cap in [[2026-08-12-fifteen-minute-weekly-review|The fifteen-minute weekly review]]. The [[2026-09-22-q4-planning-sync|Q4 planning sync]] used a thirty-minute limit and finished on time.

## What supports it

- A fixed end makes the task easier to start.
- Both uses recorded in this vault finished inside their limit.

## What argues against it

- A hard stop can cut off the part of a review that matters most. Not yet seen in this vault.

## Open questions

- Is fifteen minutes the right cap for the owner, or only for the author of the source?
