# Retrieval practice

> Fictional. This page belongs to the demo vault; the people, organisations and articles in it are invented.

## In one paragraph

Testing yourself on material instead of rereading it. In practice: close the notes, write what you remember, then check. Wrong answers show which note needs rewriting. Spaced repetition is the same idea with a schedule attached.

## Where it came from

Two sources in this vault: [[2026-09-03-retrieval-practice-at-work|Retrieval practice at work]] (English) and [[2026-09-14-oppimise-plaan|Õppimise plaan]] (Estonian). The page is in English because the two sources split one to one, and English wins a tie. The Estonian term is in the aliases.

## What supports it

- Both articles recommend recall attempts after reading, and both recommend spacing them.
- Both say a short attempt of five to ten minutes is enough, and neither gives data for that.

## What argues against it

- Neither article cites a study. The claims are the authors' own.
- The schedules differ: one, three and ten days in the English article, one, three and seven in the Estonian one. See [[review-cadence|Review cadence]].
- The owner dropped the [[morning-flashcards|morning flashcards idea]] after finding the daily load too high.
