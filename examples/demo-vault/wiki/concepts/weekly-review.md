# Weekly review

> Fictional. This page belongs to the demo vault; the people, organisations and articles in it are invented.

## In one paragraph

A short, fixed-time look back at the past week that ends in one written decision about the next. The point is not a complete record; it is catching drift early while the details are still fresh.

## Where it came from

The vault met the idea in [[2026-08-12-fifteen-minute-weekly-review|The fifteen-minute weekly review]], which caps the review at fifteen minutes. The owner's own trial of it is recorded in [[friday-review-trial|Friday review trial]] and its [[friday-review-trial-review|review]].

## What supports it

- The source argues that long reviews are skipped within a month. Not tested elsewhere in this vault.
- The trial's own evidence, in [[friday-review-trial-review|the review]]: four of four Fridays completed, each in under twenty minutes.
- The [[2026-09-22-q4-planning-sync|Q4 planning sync]] ended with written decisions, and the team found them easier to act on.

## What argues against it

- One trial of four weeks by one person says little about whether the habit lasts.
- [[retrieval-practice|Retrieval practice]] sources suggest recall attempts on a one-, three- and ten-day schedule, which a single weekly slot does not provide. See [[review-cadence|Review cadence]].
