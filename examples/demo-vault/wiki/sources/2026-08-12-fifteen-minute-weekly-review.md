# The fifteen-minute weekly review

> Fictional. This page belongs to the demo vault; the people, organisations and articles in it are invented.

## What it is

A magazine-style article by Ingrid Voll (an invented author) in an invented publication, Small Habits Quarterly. It argues that a weekly review should be capped at fifteen minutes and run on the same weekday.

## Claims

- A review that takes longer than fifteen minutes gets skipped within a month. The author offers her own experience as the evidence; the article cites no study.
- Three questions are enough: what moved, what stalled, what is next week's single priority.
- The review should end with one written decision, not a list of intentions.
- Fridays work better than Sundays because the week's details are still fresh.

## Why it matters here

It is the origin of the [[fifteen-minute-friday-review|Fifteen-minute Friday review]] idea and gives the main argument for the [[weekly-review|weekly review]] concept. Its cap on duration also relates to [[time-boxing|time-boxing]]. The claim about skipped long reviews is untested here until the [[friday-review-trial|Friday review trial]] ends.
