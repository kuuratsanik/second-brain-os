# Notes that link back (talk)

> Fictional. This page belongs to the demo vault; the people, organisations and articles in it are invented.

## What it is

A twenty-minute conference talk by Siim Tooming (an invented speaker) about why notes become useful only when each one points to at least one other. The raw file holds a cleaned transcript.

## Claims

- A note with no links in or out is a note nobody will find again.
- Write the link at the moment you write the sentence. Adding links later in a batch never happens.
- A small tool called [[margin|Margin]] shows backlinks beside the note, which the speaker says changed how he wrote.
- Folders matter less than a handful of hub pages that list what exists.

## Why it matters here

It is the support for [[note-linking|note linking]] as a concept and for keeping hubs current. It also gives one more reason to prefer a few hub pages to deep folders, which matches the structure in this vault.
