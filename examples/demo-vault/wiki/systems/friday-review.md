# Friday review

> Fictional. This page belongs to the demo vault; the people, organisations and articles in it are invented.

## What it is

A fifteen-minute review every Friday at 16:30, written by the owner.

## When and how

Calendar block, timer set to fifteen minutes. Three questions: what moved, what stalled, what is next week's single priority. The priority goes on one line at the top of the journal page for Monday.

## Why it exists

It came out of [[friday-review-trial|the Friday review trial]], justified in [[friday-review-trial-review|its review]]. The concept is [[weekly-review|Weekly review]].

## Known problems

The "what stalled" question gets one-word answers. Recall practice has no slot yet; see [[review-cadence|Review cadence]].
