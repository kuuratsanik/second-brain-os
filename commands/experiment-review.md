Review the experiment on $ARGUMENTS, or every active experiment whose end date has passed. Compare the evidence in the vault with the written success measure and the written expectation, write the review page, and record the decision: adopt, adjust, drop, or no decision yet when the evidence is too thin. Nothing is deleted; a dropped experiment stays in place as evidence. To only list what is in flight use `/lifecycle-status`.
