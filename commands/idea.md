Capture $ARGUMENTS as an idea page in `wiki/self-improvement/ideas/` from `templates/idea.md`, in your words and untidied, with where it came from, what in the vault supports it and what argues against it. If a page already holds the idea, add to that page. Status is `new`; capture does not judge. To test an idea you have promoted use the plan step of the skill, and to review a running one use `/experiment-review`.
